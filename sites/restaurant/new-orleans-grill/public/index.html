The Kitchen

Plates from the bayou, six days a week.

New Orleans Grill cooks Louisiana comfort food on Clinton Boulevard: seafood gumbo, blackened redfish, po-boys dressed the right way, and steaks straight off the grill. Breakfast starts Tuesday, lunch runs Monday through Saturday, and the kitchen stays open past dark every one of those nights too.

The doors close Sundays. Everybody deserves a day off — including the roux.

House Specialty

Red Fish Pontchartrain

Named for the lake it grew up next to. Redfish seared in a hot pan, finished with a lemon-butter sauce and a heap of lump crab on top. It sits on the House Specialties board alongside Grouper Rockefeller and Catfish Creole — order any of the three and the kitchen won't judge you for asking for extra bread.
