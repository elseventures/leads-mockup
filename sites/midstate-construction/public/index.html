Founded in Jackson in 1958, Mid-State Construction is one of Mississippi’s oldest and largest locally-owned construction firms. Three generations of owners, architects and subcontractors have trusted us with their buildings — many of them twice.

The reason is boring, and we’re proud of that: an almost unheard-of consistency in quality and efficiency, job after job, decade after decade. No drama. No surprises. No excuses riding on the invoice.

Today the toolbox includes jobsite technology our founders couldn’t have imagined — live field updates, weekly aerial photography, real-time budget and schedule reporting — but the handshake works exactly like it did in 1958.
